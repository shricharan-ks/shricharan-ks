## About

Data Scientist at **Red Hat** in Cork, Ireland, with 5+ years across AI/ML, full-stack engineering, and cloud-native infrastructure. I build end-to-end AI systems — model training and LLM pipelines through production inference on **OpenShift AI** and **Kubernetes**. My work sits where applied AI research meets enterprise engineering: agentic frameworks, LLMs at scale, and open source that's actually deployable.

- Building **Agentic AI** tooling and the **Open GenAI Stack (OGX)** for enterprise OpenShift deployments
- Knowledge tuning, model serving with **vLLM**, and agent orchestration on RHOAI
- Previously led a team of 5 at Carelon on an LLM medical-document pipeline — 89% extraction accuracy, 67% cost reduction
