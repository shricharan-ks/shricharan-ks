## About

I'm a **Data Scientist at Red Hat** in Cork, Ireland, with 5+ years across **AI/ML engineering**, full-stack development, and cloud-native infrastructure. I build end-to-end AI systems — model training and **LLM pipelines** through **production inference** on **OpenShift AI** and **Kubernetes**. My work sits where applied AI research meets enterprise engineering: **agentic AI frameworks**, **retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)**, serving large language models at scale, and open source that's actually deployable.

- Building **agentic AI** tooling and the **Open GenAI Stack (OGX)** for enterprise OpenShift deployments
- Knowledge tuning, **model serving with vLLM**, and **agent orchestration** on Red Hat OpenShift AI (RHOAI)
- Previously led a team of 5 at Carelon on an LLM medical-document pipeline — 89% extraction accuracy, 67% cost reduction
